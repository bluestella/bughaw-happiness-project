01

The governing line

Warm about people. Cold about claims.

Visually: photography and the display face carry the warmth; numbers, panels and tables stay clinical. A layout that makes a statistic look emotional is off-brand. So is a layout that makes a person look like data.

Mode rule — auto by artefact type. Decks open dark, run light, close dark. Documents stay light throughout, appendices plain. HTML artifacts take a light ground with a dark hero band. Posters and social run dark. Never mix a light-mode accent into a dark-mode layout.

03

Typography

Display — Maragsa → Gloock → Playfair Display
Hindi po basura ang problema.
Filipino copy, the brand line, pull quotes. Maragsa is Canva-only — in a generation environment you land on Gloock, and that is correct, not a compromise. Never below 24pt. Never italic. Never more than two lines.
Sans — Proxima Nova → DM Sans → Instrument Sans
Five vendors documented, Q2 2026.
Headings, body, captions, labels. Body copy at 17px in documents, 20pt floor in decks — nothing smaller, anywhere, including footnotes.
Proxima Nova is licensed; DM Sans is the free substitute that carries the web, Word and anything handed to an outside party.
Mono — IBM Plex Mono
PHP 20.00 / sack · 4.1M MT · 98.5% / 23d
Figures in tables and data annotations, where alignment matters more than warmth.
The language split is the system. Filipino display copy is set in the serif; English operational copy is set in the sans. It codes the emotional register against the operational one, so a reader can tell at a glance which one they are in.

06

Do and don't

Do

Open on the person. The story leads, the product comes last — slide 1 is never a product shot.

Let one accent run per layout: gold or brown, never both.

Print greyscale before every handover. Deep Green and Bughaw Blue converge in black and white — nothing may depend on telling those two apart.

Cut copy when a slide runs long. Forty words is the ceiling.

Don't

No gold text on cream. No bright green text on deep green. No sky blue on Bughaw blue.

No shadows, no gradients except the photographic scrim, no uniform rounded corners, no purple, no Inter.

No number without its claim pill and source. No literature figure styled as a Bughaw lab result.

No beneficiary framing — no downcast eyes, no outstretched hands. People shots show work and competence.

07

How to use it

In a new session: the skill triggers on its own for anything Bughaw-branded. Or say it plainly:

Make me a deck on the Kaagapay network for a hotel audience. Use the Bughaw theme.

Alongside theme-factory: references/bughaw.md is written in the same format as the ten built-in presets. Drop it beside them and Bughaw becomes the eleventh.

Apply the Bughaw theme to this deck.

In an HTML artifact: paste references/tokens.css at the top. Every variable and component primitive is there.

In Canva or with a designer: hand over tokens.json and layouts.md. Both are readable without this system loaded.
